Constitution Highlights

The Principles That Govern Us

Every operation, entitlement and initiative strictly follows our binding Constitution — amended only by majority vote of the members.

🕌
01

Shariah-Compliant Investment

Funds are invested only in interest-free (halal), profitable sectors — focusing on building & housing construction — upon formal Investment Board decisions.

🛡️
02

The “Amanat” Principle

All member deposits are held as a sacred trust. Under no circumstances shall any loan be issued to a member from these deposited funds.

⚖️
03

Equal Profit & Loss Sharing

Every member is an equal stakeholder in net profits and losses, based on investment ratio and proportionate duration of investment.

🏦
04

Banking Discipline

All subscriptions are deposited into the official bank account; deposit receipts are recorded in the register at every monthly meeting. Chequebook custody stays with a non-signatory.

🗳️
05

Elected Governance

Executive & Advisory Councils are directly elected every 2 years, with a joint Investment Board headed by the Chief Advisor.

🕊️
06

Welfare First

From bereavement support to voluntary mutual aid — human welfare remains the heartbeat of every decision.

Three-Tier Governance

Structured. Accountable. Elected.

🧭

Advisory Council

উপদেষ্টা পরিষদ

Guides policy, oversees overall affairs, designates bank signatories and safeguards the Constitution.

⚙️

Executive Council

কার্যনির্বাহী পরিষদ

Executes daily operations, holds mandatory meetings every 30 days and manages all society affairs.

📈

Investment Board

বিনিয়োগ বোর্ড

A joint board of both councils, headed by the Chief Advisor, deciding all halal investment destinations.

Membership

Join the Family of Guardians

Open to every permanent citizen of Bangladesh who pledges unconditional adherence to the society’s Constitution.

✅ Eligibility & Documents

  • ✓ Permanent citizen of Bangladesh.
  • ✓ Pledge to follow all rules & regulations of the Constitution.
  • ✓ 1 photocopy of National ID / Voter ID card.
  • ✓ 2 passport-size photographs of the applicant.
  • ✓ 1 passport-size photograph of the nominee.
  • ✓ Prescribed membership form addressed to the President.
  • ✓ Notify the society immediately upon any address change.
💳 Non-refundable application form fee: BDT 200
01
Submit Application

Complete the prescribed Membership Form addressed to the President with all attachments.

02
Pay Admission Fee

Pay the BDT 200 form fee and attach NID copy, photographs and nominee details.

03
Verification & Approval

The Executive Council reviews, verifies and formally approves your enrollment with a Membership ID.

04
Enjoy Full Rights

Full voting rights in elections and proportionate profit/loss sharing from your joining date.

Welfare & Discipline

Built on Trust, Run with Discipline

Checks and balances at every level — because your Amanat is our sacred responsibility.

🕯️

Bereavement Welfare

Upon the death of an active member, fellow members may voluntarily support the bereaved family with BDT 200/month per member — never forced, always from the heart.

📅

Monthly Meetings

The Executive Council holds a mandatory formal meeting every 30 days — deposits recorded, resolutions documented, minutes preserved.

🔐

Checks & Balances

Withdrawals require a joint council resolution with exact purpose & cheque number. The chequebook rests with a non-signatory member or the Chief Advisor.
